New to Soundtrack?

It's not Spotify with a business logo.

Soundtrack is a streaming service built specifically for businesses. It's fully licensed for public play — which a personal Spotify or Apple Music account is not, even though plenty of venues run on one anyway. Underneath, there's a catalog of well over 100 million songs, scheduling by time of day, and one dashboard to control every location, if you have more than one.

What it doesn't do on its own is know your room. That's the part we handle.
